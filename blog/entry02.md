## Hardware

After Completing the software leg of my freedom project, I started to look at Hardware. Hardware is really important, because tools like computers and machines help architects and workers with the design and construction of a project. For example, you need a good CPU for modeling and processing designs and projects, especially when making CAD models (Computer-Aided Design). This is where the first hardware I researched comes in. The first product I looked at was CPUs by AMD. https://www.amd.com/en/products/workstations/architecture-and-engineering.html AMD has a lineup of specially designed CPUs meant for tasks like these. On their website, they several different CPUs and Processors for architectural-based work, such as the AMD Ryzen Threadripper™ lineup of processors.
